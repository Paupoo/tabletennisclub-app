# Equipment Management

This guide explains how to track members who hold club equipment: key rings and cash registers.

---

## Key holders

### Why?

Some members hold a key ring that opens and closes the venue. This helps you know who to contact at events.

### Assigning a key

1. Go to **Admin → Members** and open the member's profile.
2. In the **Entrusted equipment** section, check **Has a key**.
3. Save.

### Removing a key

Same process: uncheck **Has a key** and save.

---

## Cash registers

### Why?

Each club register is entrusted to a member (usually the treasurer or a delegate). This lets you know who is responsible for each register at any time.

### Creating a register with a holder

1. Go to **Admin → Treasury → Cash Register**.
2. Click **New register**.
3. Enter the register name and select the holder.
4. Click **Create**.

> Only **administrators** and **treasurers** can do this.

### Changing a register's holder

1. Go to **Admin → Treasury → Cash Register**.
2. Select the register (if more than one exists).
3. Next to the current holder's name, click **Change**.
4. Pick the new holder from the list.
5. Click **Save**.

> Only **administrators** and **treasurers** can do this.

---

## Equipment overview

For a quick summary of who holds what:

1. Go to **Admin → Club Information**.
2. Scroll to the **Equipment holders** section.

You will find:
- The list of members with a key
- For each register: its name and the member who holds it

---

## Filtering the member list

In **Admin → Members**, two toggle filters are available:

| Filter | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Has a key** | Shows only members who hold a key |
| **Has a cash register** | Shows only members who hold a register |

To activate: click the **Filters** button (funnel icon, top-right), then toggle the filter on.
