# Managing entrusted equipment

This guide explains how to track club property handed over to people: key rings and cash registers.

---

## Key rings

### Why?

A key ring opens and closes the venue. The club owns a finite number of them: some are in a member's pocket, others wait in the drawer. The inventory answers one question, but the right one: **on the evening of an event, who do I call?**

Every key ring carries a **number assigned automatically**, and that number is never reused. Retiring a ring therefore leaves a permanent gap: the club's n°3 stays the only n°3 in its history.

> Holding a key ring **opens no screen** in the application. It records a fact; it is not a permission.

### Where?

**Administration → Club Settings → Key rings**.

The screen lists one ring per row: its number, its holder (or "In the drawer" when nobody has it) and any notes.

> This screen is reserved for holders of the **Facilities** delegation and for **administrators**.

### Creating a key ring

1. Click **Create a key ring**.
2. The number is assigned on its own — there is nothing to type.
3. Optionally pick a holder — leave it empty to keep the ring in the drawer.
4. Optionally add a note (what the ring opens, where it is kept).
5. Click **Create**.

### Moving a key ring

1. On the ring's row, click **Move**.
2. Search for the new holder by name.
3. Click **Move**.

Only **active members** appear in the search: an entrusted object does not go to someone who has left the club. Leave the field empty to put the ring back in the drawer.

If the current holder is no longer active, they stay shown on the row — which is precisely the ring to collect.

### Retiring a key ring

1. On the ring's row, click **Retire**.
2. Confirm.

The ring leaves the list but is **not erased**: it keeps its number and its last holder. That is how you know whose pocket a missing ring went astray from.

### Putting a key ring back in service

1. Tick **Show retired key rings** above the list.
2. On the greyed-out row, click **Put back in service**.

## Cash registers

### Why?

Each of the club's cash registers is entrusted to a member (usually the treasurer or a delegate), so that responsibility for every register is known at all times.

### Creating a register with a holder

1. Go to **Administration → Treasury → Cash Register**.
2. Click **New register**.
3. Enter the register name and search for the holder by name.
4. Click **Create**.

> This action is reserved for **administrators** and **treasurers**.

### Changing a register's holder

1. Go to **Administration → Treasury → Cash Register**.
2. Select the register concerned (if there are several).
3. Next to the current holder's name, click **Change**.
4. Search for the new holder by name.
5. Click **Save**.

> This action is reserved for **administrators** and **treasurers**.

Changing the holder **does not settle the register and moves no money**: it only moves the displayed responsibility.

### Retiring a cash register

1. Go to **Administration → Treasury → Cash Register** and select the register.
2. Click **Retire**, then confirm.

The register leaves the list but **its ledger is kept in full**: not a single movement is erased. Tick **Show retired registers** to find it again, and **Put back in service** to reactivate it.

## Equipment overview

To see who holds what at a glance:

1. Go to **Administration → Club Information**.
2. Scroll down to the **Equipment holders** section.

You will find:
- Every key ring with its holder, including the ones in the drawer
- For each register: the register name and the member who holds it

Retired key rings are not listed there.

> This page is reserved for holders of the **Supervision** delegation and for **administrators**.

---

## Filtering the member list

In **Administration → Members**, two filters are available:

| Filter | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Has a key** | Shows only members holding at least one key ring |
| **Holds a cash register** | Shows only members holding a register |

To turn a filter on: click **Filters** (the funnel icon, top right) and enable the filter you want.

---

## Reading the history

Every creation, move and retirement of a key ring or a register is recorded in the audit log, with its author and date.

Open **Audit** in the menu, then filter on the **Key ring** or **Cash register** model.

> The audit log is reserved for holders of the **Supervision** delegation and for **administrators**.
