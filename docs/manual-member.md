# Member User Manual

This manual covers everything a club member can do in the app.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Receiving your invitation

The club secretary creates your account and sends you an invitation email. The email contains a button **"Finaliser mon inscription"**. Click it within **48 hours** — the link expires after that. If it expires, contact the secretary to resend it.

### Setting your password

When you click the invitation link, you land on a page to set your password. Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters, at least one letter, one number, one symbol). Once saved, you are automatically logged in and receive a welcome email.

### Logging in

Go to the app URL and click **Login**. Enter your email address and password. Tick **Remember me** to stay logged in on your device.

### Logging out

Click your avatar or name in the top navigation bar, then **Logout**.

### Forgotten password

On the login page, click **Forgot your password?**, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

---

## 2. My Profile

Access via **My space → My profile** in the navigation.

### What you can edit

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| Identity | First name, last name, gender, date of birth, photo |
| Contact | Email, phone number, address, city |
| Financial | IBAN (for refunds) |
| Guardian | Emergency contact phone (for minors) |

### Updating your profile

Click **Edit profile** (pencil icon or drawer). Modify the fields and click **Save**. Changes take effect immediately.

### Uploading a photo

In the profile edit drawer, click the photo area or drag-and-drop an image (JPG, PNG, WebP, max 2 MB). Click **Save** to confirm. To remove your photo, click the trash icon next to the current photo.

### Changing your email

Update your email in the profile edit form. After saving, a verification email is sent to your new address. Your account remains active during this time.

### Changing your password

Go to **My space → Settings → Security**. Click **Change password**, enter your current password and the new one twice.

### GDPR — Requesting account deletion

At the bottom of your profile page, in the **Danger zone** section, click **Request account deletion**. This sends a request to the administrator. Your data will be anonymized (name, email, phone, address replaced with anonymous placeholders). Relational data (tournament results, payment history) may be kept for accounting purposes.

---

## 3. My Settings

Access via **My space → Settings**.

### Appearance

Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, or **Auto** (follows your device theme). The change applies immediately and is saved to your profile.

---

## 4. My Teams

Access via **My space → Teams**.

Shows all interclub teams you belong to. For each team you can see:
- Team name and division
- Season
- Your teammates and the captain

You cannot modify team membership yourself — this is managed by the committee.

---

## 5. My Calendar

Access via **My space → Calendar**.

Displays all upcoming events you are registered for: tournaments, interclub matches, meetings, training sessions. Use the calendar view to see your schedule at a glance.

---

## 6. Event Subscriptions & Payments

Access via **My space → Event subscriptions**.

### Viewing pending payments

The page shows all your pending payments (tournament registrations, training packs, etc.) with the amount due.

### Paying for an event

Click **Pay** next to a pending payment. A payment modal opens with:
- Amount due
- IBAN of the club
- A QR code for mobile banking apps (scan to prefill the transfer)
- Your structured communication reference (mandatory — use it exactly as shown)

Make the bank transfer manually. The treasurer reconciles payments and marks yours as paid.

### RSVP for meetings

If you are invited to a club meeting, your invitation appears here. Click **RSVP** to confirm your attendance. You may also indicate whether you want to reserve a meal (if applicable). The committee sees your response automatically.

---

## 7. Registration Management

Access via **My space → Registration management**.

### Club affiliation (membership)

To affiliate for the current season, click **Affiliate**. Choose your membership type (recreational or competitive). If you are a competitive player, your licence and ranking are required (set by the committee). Once submitted, the secretary processes your registration.

**Note for minors:** If you are under 18, a guardian (parent or legal tutor) must be registered in the system. If none is recorded, the affiliation is blocked. Contact the secretary.

### Training packs

Browse available training sessions. Click **Subscribe** to request enrollment. The committee approves or rejects your request. You receive a notification by email with the result.

### Document uploads

Upload your **medical certificate** and, if you are under 18, your **parental consent form**. These are required before your affiliation can be confirmed.

---

## 8. Tournaments

### Viewing upcoming tournaments

Published tournaments appear on the tournaments page. Each card shows: name, date, location, price, maximum players, and available spots.

### Registering for a tournament

Click **Register** on the tournament page. If registration is open and spots are available, you are added to the participant list. If the tournament is full, you are added to the waiting list and notified when a spot opens.

### Tournament payments

After registering, a payment request appears in **My event subscriptions**. Pay by bank transfer using the provided QR code and communication reference.

### Viewing results

After a tournament closes, results and rankings are published on the tournament page.

### Unregistering

If registration is still open, you can unregister from the tournament page. You receive a confirmation email. Refunds follow the club's refund policy.

---

## 9. Interclub (Competitions)

### My matches

Access via **My space → Calendar** or the interclub section. See your scheduled interclub matches, including date, opponent, and venue.

### Availability

Before each interclub round, the captain may request your availability. Respond **Available / Unavailable** directly from the email notification or from the interclub section.

### Selection

When you are selected for a lineup, you receive a selection notification by email. The lineup is published before the match.

---

## 10. Notifications

The app sends email notifications for:
- Invitation to set your password (onboarding)
- Welcome email after first login
- Tournament registration confirmation
- Tournament payment requests and reminders
- Waitlist status updates (added, promoted, spot opened)
- Tournament cancellation or updates
- Meeting invitations and RSVPs
- Training pack approval/rejection
- Interclub availability requests and selection
- Refund requests

You cannot currently manage notification preferences per-type. To stop receiving emails, contact the administrator.

---

## 11. Help & Contact

If you have a problem:
- Contact your club secretary directly
- Use the **Contact form** on the club website (public page)
- Ask the committee at the next training session

There is no in-app help chat at this time.
