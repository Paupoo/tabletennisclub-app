# Selector / Interclub Committee Manual

This manual explains how a selector or committee member uses the global selections view to oversee all teams and step in when needed. For captain-level features (making a team selection, sending the lineup), refer to the [Captain Manual](manual-captain.md).

---

## 1. Access and permissions

The selections view is available to:
- **Captains** — see only their own team(s)
- **Selectors** (`is_selector`) — see all club teams, can make selections for any team and search for substitutes across all club players
- **Committee members / Administrators** — same access as selectors

Access the page via **Interclub → Selections**.

---

## 2. Global view — all teams

As a selector, all teams for the current season are visible. Use the **Filters** button (top right) to focus on a specific team.

Each team displays:
- Its **name** and **division** (badge)
- The **captain's name**
- The list of its matches with their status

---

## 3. Understanding statuses

Each match carries a status shown as a **coloured bar** on the left of the row.

| Colour | Status | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | **Confirmed** | Lineup sent — nothing to do |
| 🟡 Orange | **Actionable** | Enough players available or selection complete, but the lineup has not been sent yet. Chase the captain or send it yourself. |
| 🔴 Red | **Urgent** | Match within 14 days, not enough availability responses. Act now. |
| ⚪ Light grey | **Upcoming** | Match is far away, no urgency |
| ◼ Dark grey | **Past** | Match has been played |

The **X/Y avail.** counter (shown in grey on the right of each row) indicates how many players have confirmed availability out of the total required.

---

## 4. Urgent alert banner

If any match across any team is **Urgent**, a **red banner** appears at the top of the page. It lists all affected matches with their available player counts.

Click a match in the banner to open its selection drawer directly — without having to scroll to the relevant team.

> This banner is your priority dashboard. At the start of each week, make sure it is empty.

---

## 5. Prioritising actions

Recommended order of priority on each visit:

1. **Red banner** — handle all **Urgent** matches within 14 days immediately
2. **Orange status** — check that the captain is aware; send the lineup yourself if the captain is unavailable
3. **Light grey (Upcoming)** — verify that availability requests have been sent for matches coming up soon

---

## 6. Stepping in on a selection

As a selector, you can open the selection drawer for any match, regardless of which team it belongs to.

Click **Select** on the match row. The process is identical to the [Captain Manual](manual-captain.md#4-making-the-selection), with one additional feature.

### Searching for a substitute

At the bottom of the selection drawer, a **Search a substitute** section is available exclusively to selectors and administrators.

Type at least 2 characters of a player's name to search across **all competitive club members**, not just the usual team roster. Results appear below the search field. Click a player to add them to the selection.

> A player found through search can be selected even if they are not in the team's regular roster. Verify manually that they are available and informed.

A player already lined up in another team for that same match day is flagged with the relevant team name (lock icon 🔒) — they cannot be selected twice.

---

## 7. Sending the lineup on behalf of the captain

After making or editing a selection, click **Save selection**. The send window opens. You can add a meetup message, then send. The email is sent to the whole team regardless of who made the selection.

---

## 8. Switching seasons

The season selector at the top right lets you browse selection history from previous seasons, in read-only mode.

---

## 9. What this view does not cover

- **It does not manage official match sheets** — that goes through the federation's own tools
- **It does not track match results** — use the **Results** section for that
- **It does not send automatic reminders** — follow-ups are manual (envelope button on each match row)
